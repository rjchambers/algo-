Takeaway: the strategy that wins the backtest is chosen BY the backtest;
that is selection bias, and it does not carry forward. A 'reverse-engineered
winner' is a description of the past, not a prediction of the future.

Theory: TSMOM is one of the few anomalies documented across decades and asset
classes (Moskowitz/Ooi/Pedersen 2012). Long-biased + vol-targeted to respect
crypto's upward drift and avoid shorting bull trends.
